# Application tracker: archive

Closed-out rows, moved here from `tracker.md`. **Nothing is deleted. Every row below
is the full, verbatim row as it stood in `tracker.md`**, so the record of what was
sent, when, and what came back is unchanged.

`tracker.md` holds only what is still actionable, which is what keeps it short
enough to read at the start of every session. **The row moves in the same edit that
records the outcome**: whoever writes "rejected" into the tracker moves the row here
and adds its index line. Asking for "an archive pass" sweeps up anything that was
missed, in `tracker.md` and `leads.md` both.

## What lives here

Four outcomes, none of them actionable. They are the four closing statuses in
`tracker.md`:

- **rejected**: the employer said no, in writing. Quote their reason.
- **gone quiet**: submitted, acknowledged or not, and the follow-up date passed by a
  week or more with nothing back. **These are NOT rejections.** Nobody has said no.
  They are parked because they are not actionable, and if one ever answers, move the
  row back into `tracker.md` rather than replying from here. When a rejection does
  arrive for a parked row, update the outcome here and keep both dates.
- **closed before sending**: the requisition closed while the application was still
  being prepared, so nothing was ever submitted.
- **withdrawn**: you pulled the application, or decided not to send something that
  was drafted. Say which, and why.

Live applications, drafted-but-unsent applications, and anything whose follow-up
date is still ahead stay in `tracker.md`.

## Dedupe index

**This table exists so that `discover` and `outreach` can check history cheaply,
without reading the full rows below.** A company appearing here has already been
approached: read its full row before approaching it again.

- For **advertised roles**, a rejection on one requisition does not bar a different
  one at the same company. A second application to the *same* requisition is the
  mistake.
- For **outreach**, a company that rejected you is dropped unless the rejection
  invited a later approach, and a `gone quiet` row counts as a live relationship:
  skip it, or raise it first.

The reasoning for every outcome is in the full row.
