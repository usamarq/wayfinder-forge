# Landscape: the researched academic map

Everything you have learned about who is doing the work you want to do, who funds
it, and what the routes in actually are. `outreach-academic` reads this before
approaching anyone.

**CLAUDE.md hard rule 7 applies to this whole file: names and relationships are
never guessed.** A title, a group membership, or a project affiliation goes in here
because it was read on a university page (with the URL and the date) or because you
said so. Nothing here is inferred from a publication list or a job title.
