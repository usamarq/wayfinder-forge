# Funding and deadline calendar

**Open every session by checking this file.** Anything inside three weeks gets
surfaced before other work. A SessionStart hook reads the dates in the tables below
and raises them automatically, but the hook is a safety net and not a substitute for
looking.

Grant windows do not reopen for six or twelve months. A missed one costs a year, not
a week.

## The rule for every row in this file

**Every deadline, amount and eligibility claim states a source URL and the date it
was verified.** A fact that cannot be verified on a live page is marked
`UNVERIFIED`. A remembered date is treated as wrong until re-verified.

This is not pedantry. Funders move deadlines, change eligibility between rounds, and
publish different dates on their English and local-language pages. The version that
counts is the one on the live call page today.

Dates are written `YYYY-MM-DD` with the year always present, plus the closing time
and timezone where the call states one. "15 October" is not a deadline; it is half
of one.
