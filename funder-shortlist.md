# Funder shortlist

The screened list of funders worth approaching, in the terms a supervisor or a head
of group will actually ask about.

Three questions decide whether a funder is worth an application, and they are the
three an experienced academic will ask you within a minute of you naming one:

1. **Topic fit.** Do they fund work like yours, evidenced by what they have actually
   funded, not by what their mission statement says.
2. **Previous grants awarded.** How many, to whom, at what career stage. A
   foundation that funded four doctoral researchers in your field last year is a
   different proposition from one that funded forty senior projects.
3. **Total amount distributed.** The headline maximum is marketing; the median award
   is the number that matters.

Anything not confirmed against a live page is marked `NOT CONFIRMED`, and it stays
marked until it is.
