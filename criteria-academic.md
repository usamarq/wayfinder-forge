## Scoring rubric (1 to 5 each; drop below 3 overall)

1. **Line fit**: Tier 1 direct > Tier 2 with a stated rationale > generic.
2. **Supervisor and group quality**: publication activity in the line, people
   verified on university pages, never guessed.
3. **Funding solidity and duration**: fully funded 3 to 4 years > 1 to 2 years >
   unfunded study right.
4. **Deadline feasibility**: enough runway for an honest application. A sprint is a
   deliberate choice, not a default.
5. **Career value**: does it build the identity you want to have in three years.

Any auto-drop gate: drop regardless of score, one-line reason.
