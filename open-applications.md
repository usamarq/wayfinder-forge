# Open applications and cold-email targets

Companies worth approaching with no advertised role. `outreach` reads and updates
this file.

An open application only makes sense where there is **no live matching role**. If a
company is advertising something you could do, that is a normal application and it
goes to `tailor` instead. Sending a spontaneous application to a company that is
actively advertising a suitable role reads as not having looked.

**Statuses**: `candidate` (worth researching) -> `researched` (channel and hook
found) -> `drafted` (staged for you to send) -> `sent` (add a `tracker.md` row) ->
`replied` / `no reply` / `skip` (with a reason).

**Channel tiers** (recorded per company, because it predicts the reply rate):

1. A dedicated open-application form the company published. Strongest: they invited it.
2. A published recruitment address (`careers@`, `jobs@`, `recruitment@`).
3. A named recruiter or hiring contact the company itself published.
4. Generic `info@` / `hello@`. Weak, and say so.
