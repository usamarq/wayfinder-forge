# Tracker: academic applications and outreach

One row per academic application, grant proposal, or outreach email in flight.

**Statuses**: `drafted` -> `staged` (complete, awaiting your send or submit) ->
`sent-by-you` / `submitted-by-you` -> `acknowledged` -> `shortlisted` ->
`interviewing` -> `offer` / `rejected` / `no reply`.

The `-by-you` statuses are deliberate. Nothing in this repo sends or submits, so a
row never advances past `staged` on its own.
